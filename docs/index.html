MATH3085/6143: Survival Models

Welcome to MATH3085/6143: Survival Models! MATH3085/6143 introduces the fundamental concepts and key issues in lifetime and time-to-event data analysis, with applications in areas such as actuarial practice, medical sciences, and demography.

Note that MATH3085 and MATH6143 are double-badged. This means that the course content of the two modules is the same but some of the assessment differs.

The Panopto recorded lectures are available on Blackboard (links for MATH3085 and MATH6143; note that you must be logged in to access them).

📝 Assessment

The basic module assessment breakdown is

  1. 30% data analysis project
  2. 70% written exam

The data analysis project consists of a series of tasks in which you will conduct survival analyses on the provided datasets using R. The project will be released in early December 2026, with detailed instructions and guidance provided towards the end of the term.

📖 Lecture Notes

Lecture notes are available in two formats:

  1. Skeleton notes: containing omissions corresponding to mathematical details.
  2. Complete notes: in which the previously omitted parts are included and highlighted in blue.

Students are encouraged to use whichever version of the notes they find most helpful to their learning.

📒 Syllabus

The module content is structured into 16 chapters (with the corresponding slides updated throughout the term)

  1. Introduction
  2. Statistical Models
  3. The Survival Distribution
  4. Distributions for Survival Models
  5. Survival models: parameter estimation
  6. Non-parametric Survival Estimation
  7. Survival Regression Models
  8. Multistate Survival Models
  9. Inference for Multistate Models
  10. Modelling Human Lifetime
  11. The Life Table and Life Expectancy
  12. Interpolating a Life Table
  13. Life Table Models
  14. Exposure to Risk
  15. Comparing Mortality Rates
  16. Graduation
